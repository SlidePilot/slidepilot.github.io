## 6. The SlidePilot app

**SlidePilot collects no data.** The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no server.

- **Your files:** PDFs, notes, drawings and settings stay on your device. They leave it only when you export them yourself.
- **Remote control:** Remote control connects two of your devices directly and encrypted over the local network. Slide images, notes and commands are sent only between these devices, never through a server. The devices find each other via Bonjour and show each other their device names. The keys of paired devices are stored in your device’s keychain.
- **Purchases:** The in-app purchase SlidePilot Pro is handled entirely by Apple’s App Store. The app only asks the App Store whether the purchase belongs to your Apple Account and receives no payment details.
- **Crash reports:** If you agreed in your device settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to me. The app itself sends nothing.
